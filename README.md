# atnet

Fitting the effects of antimalarial-treated nets (ATNs) on *Plasmodium
falciparum* development in *Anopheles* mosquitoes to laboratory assay data, and
projecting their impact on malaria.

Two effects of exposure to an ATN are fitted in Stan: blocking of infection
(before and after an infectious blood meal), and lengthening of the extrinsic
incubation period (EIP). The fitted effects parameterise a customised version
of [malariasimulation](https://github.com/andrewcglover/malariasimulation)
(branch `atn-dev`), which projects the impact of ATNs and of nets co-treated
with insecticides.

**Status:** under development.

## Layout

| Folder | Contents |
|---|---|
| `R/` | package functions |
| `inst/stan/` | Stan models |
| `tests/testthat/` | tests |
| `analysis/` | scripts for the paper (not part of the installed package) |
| `data_private/` | laboratory data (not in this repository, see below) |
| `outputs/` | fits, tables and figures (not in this repository) |

## Data

The laboratory assay data were generated by the Catteruccia group (Harvard T.H.
Chan School of Public Health) and are not yet public. The scripts expect them
in `data_private/`, which is excluded from this repository.
