# Where the fitted data come from

The laboratory data were generated by the Catteruccia group (Harvard T.H. Chan
School of Public Health) and are not in this repository. The scripts expect
them in `data_private/`, arranged as below. This file records which workbooks
feed which fit, and every correction applied on the way, so that any number in
the paper can be traced to a cell in a spreadsheet.

## Folders

| Folder | Contents |
|---|---|
| `data_private/fitting/` | the three workbooks the fits are built from |
| `data_private/archive/` | every earlier version and superseded summary |

Files in `fitting/` are named for when the email carrying them was received, in
UK time, so the data behind a fit can always be traced to a message.

## The two workbook layouts

These are organised oppositely, which is the source of most of the difficulty:

- **per-replicate** (`individualreps`): one worksheet per replicate, that is one
  date and one infection, with the conditions side by side as columns;
- **pooled** (`pooledreps`): one worksheet per experimental design, with
  replicates stacked vertically, and in places concatenated within a single
  column.

Neither suffices alone. The per-replicate workbook gives the true replicate
boundaries but is missing one replicate; the pooled workbook holds everything
but merges replicates within some columns.

## Readings that are not plain readings of the workbooks

Each is applied in code, reported in the output, and listed by
`assay_corrections()`.

| Correction | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Worksheet `6h 72h 2BF post 10.9.25 spz13` is one experiment, not three. Its staggered blank cells read as three bands when the sheet is read across. | Saxena, 27 Apr 2026 15:23 |
| In `#5 6h 72h post int`, the first two groups of the 72h one-blood-meal columns are one experiment. | Saxena, 27 Apr 2026 16:29 |
| In `6h 72h 2BF post 11.6.25 spz13`, the one-blood-meal columns hold the same 24 values twice; count them once. | identical values, verified |
| `72h 2BF post 09.07.26 spz10` holds a copy of `6h 72h 2BF post 11.3.25 spz10`; ignore it, and take the genuine replicate from pooled `#7 72h post spz10`. | identical values, verified; the pooled workbook reconciles only on this reading |
| The dissection day is taken from the worksheet name, not from a typed column. | colour coding in the pooled workbook links the affected rows to a day 13 worksheet |
| Only nets at 200 mg per square metre are fitted. | decision, A C Glover, 9 Oct 2026 |
| Only the standard six-minute rest on the net is fitted. | model scope; the manuscript draft's "standard 6-minute exposure time" |

The last two exclude the `100mg` column of `#1 0h int` and the three and one
minute arms of `#2 6,3,1 min exposure pre`. Both are real data; neither is
something the model can represent.

## The two tables

`analysis/02_build_fitting_data.R` writes both into `outputs/`, dated.

`<date>_sporozoite_experiments.csv` has one row per experiment, for the fit to
the extrinsic incubation period:

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `experiment_id` | 1 upwards, the index the fit gives its per-experiment effect |
| `worksheet`, `control_column`, `treated_column`, `workbook` | where the row came from |
| `hours_after_infection` | when the mosquitoes met the net, counted forward from the infectious blood meal |
| `dissection_day` | day the salivary glands were examined, from the worksheet name |
| `n_bloodmeals` | one, or two where a second blood meal followed exposure |
| `duration_min` | how long they rested on the net, where the worksheet varied it |
| `n_control`, `n_positive_control`, `n_treated`, `n_positive_treated` | dissected and infected in each arm |

`<date>_oocyst_mosquitoes.csv` has one row per mosquito, for the
transmission-blocking fit, which works on burdens rather than on counts of
infected mosquitoes. It shares the columns above where they apply, and adds
`arm`, `concentration`, `oocysts` (that mosquito's burden) and `subgroup` (its
replicate's position down the column).

Both carry a long-named `experiment (...)` column repeating the fields that
define an experiment, so a row can be read on its own.

**Sign of `hours_after_infection`.** Positive means the mosquitoes were already
infected when they met the net; negative means they met it first. This is the
same quantity as the `post_time` column of the files these fits were previously
given, and follows the supplementary information.

The earlier oocyst file carried the same number twice, as `post_time` and as
its negative `pre_time`, and the blocking model was fitted from the latter. So
code reading that model's input expects the opposite sign to the column here,
and must take `pre_time` as `-hours_after_infection`, or set its `side` and `s`
from this column directly.

## Reconciliation

The pooled and per-replicate workbooks must agree on how many mosquitoes exist
for each condition once the corrections are applied. Both days balance exactly:
239 mosquitoes for the 72 hour exposures read at day 13, and 212 at day 10. The
cleaning step checks this and stops if a future workbook fails to balance.
